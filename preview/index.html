02

Engineering

It has never been easier to generate a tool that almost works. Documents people rely on deserve better than almost.

R-01

Measured, not asserted

Published figures come from measurement scripts, and guard tests fail the build when the page and the code disagree.

R-02

Adversarial by routine

More than 6,400 tests across the family, adversarial batteries thrown at the raw wire, and suites run in both orders before a release.

R-03

Verified writes

Backups before every mutation, verification after every write, zero corruptions across the fixture corpus.

R-04

Refuses rather than guesses

A confident wrong edit is the one product we do not stock. Refusals name the real cause and the safe route when one exists.

R-05

Honest limits

Capabilities that do not exist are named. Skipped tests say why. Changelogs say what actually changed.

R-06

Built by its first customer

Every server runs daily in the maker's own work long before it ships. The rough edges get found at our desk, not yours.
