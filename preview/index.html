Didymoi

Ask a model the same question twice and you get two different answers. Didymoi makes AI-assisted analysis reproducible: a deterministic layer builds the record, and the model works inside it without the power to alter it. Grounding handed over as a fence, not a springboard.

Same data, same model, only the handoff changed: consistency rose from 0.75 to 0.95 on a 108-source benchmark. A way of making a probabilistic system answerable to a deterministic one.

How we work

It has never been easier to generate a tool that almost works. Documents people rely on deserve better than almost.

R-01

Measured, not asserted

Every number we publish comes from a measurement, and the build fails when a claim and the code disagree. Marketing does not get a vote.

R-02

We break it first

Nothing ships because a demo worked once. It ships after we have tried to break it deliberately, repeatedly, and on the record.

R-03

Done means verified

A change is not done when it runs; it is done when it is checked after it lands. Backups first, receipts after, every time.

R-04

Honest over impressive

A confident wrong answer is the one thing we refuse to ship. When something cannot be done right, we say so and say why.

R-05

Limits in writing

What we cannot do is stated, not hidden. Skipped tests name their reasons. Changelogs say what actually changed.

R-06

Built by its first customer

Everything here runs daily in the maker's own work long before it ships. The rough edges get found at our desk, not yours.

The business

Open source where it builds trust, licensed where it captures value. The servers are AGPL-3.0 and free for individuals, forever. A company building them into a product of its own buys a commercial license; terms are worked out case by case. That is the whole model, and it is already in writing on every repository.
