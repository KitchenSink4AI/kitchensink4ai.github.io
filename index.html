03

How we work

It has never been easier to ship a tool that almost works. Almost is the whole problem. We build the ones that just work.

R-01

Measured, not asserted

Every number comes off a measurement script, and the build fails the moment the code stops backing it. Marketing does not get a vote.

R-02

We break it first

Nothing ships off one good demo. It ships after we have attacked it deliberately, repeatedly, and on the record.

R-03

Done means verified

Running is not done. Verified is done. Backups first, receipts after, every time.

R-04

Honest over impressive

A confident wrong answer is the one thing we refuse to ship. When something cannot be done right, it says so, and says why.

R-05

Limits in writing

What we cannot do is written down. Skips name their reasons. Changelogs name what actually changed.

R-06

Built by its first customer

All of it runs daily in the founder's own work before anyone else touches it. The rough edges die at our desk, not yours.

04

The business

Open where it builds trust, licensed where it captures value. The servers are AGPL-3.0 and free under its terms, forever; that is the funnel. A company building them into a product of its own buys a commercial license; that is the revenue. The model is simple, already in writing on every repository, and it scales with every integration.
